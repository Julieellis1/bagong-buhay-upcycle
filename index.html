Old fabric.
New life.

Bagong Buhay is Kim’s slow-fashion label: one-of-a-kind clothes, totes and bags sewn by hand in small batches from 100% second-hand fabric.

Where the fabric comes from

Every piece starts as something someone else let go.

“estate sales, Goodwill, friends’ closets, a 2nd-hand Kimono shop in Japan …”

Kim, on where her fabric comes from · Instagram, July 2025
  1. 01

    Found

    Fabric, remnants and worn-out favourites from estate sales, thrift racks and friends’ closets.

  2. 02

    Reworked

    Cut, pieced and stitched by Kim in small batches. Seams and mends stay visible on purpose.

  3. 03

    One of one

    Each piece exists once. When it finds a home, it’s gone. That’s the point.
