Care & FAQ

Look after it, and it keeps living.

Second-hand fabric has already proven it can last. A little care keeps it going.

Wash

Cold, inside out, gentle cycle. Hand-wash delicate chiffon and kimono fabrics.

Dry

Line or flat dry. Skip the hot dryer. Mixed fabrics shrink at different rates.

Press

Low heat with a cloth over patches and printed scraps.

Mend

Catch small holes early. A visible mend is a feature here, not a flaw.

Questions

What does “Bagong Buhay” mean?

It’s Tagalog for “new life”. Kim chose it because her mission is to give old materials new life.

Who makes everything?

Kim. Every piece is made by her, by hand, in small batches.

What is it made from?

100% second-hand fabric — from estate sales, Goodwill, friends’ closets, a second-hand kimono shop in Japan, and more.

Is every piece one of a kind?

Yes. Bagong Buhay is a slow-fashion brand focused on 1/1 pieces, so when one is gone, it’s gone.

How do I buy a piece?

In person at markets, or by sending Kim a message on Instagram. You can also send a hold request from the Pieces page.

Why are there no prices?

Each piece is priced individually. Ask on Instagram or send a hold request and Kim will reply with details.

Is there an online shop?

Not right now. Market tables and Instagram DMs are the way to buy.

Where are you based?

Hillsboro, Oregon — handmade in the Portland area.

When is the next market?

None announced yet. Follow @bagongbuhayupcycle or join the drop list to hear first.

What is a patch party?

Bring clothing or a fabric accessory, pick a patch, and sew it on — Kim ran one at the Belmont Street Fair with @craftea.fg.

Can Kim rework something I already own?

Maybe. Use the “give it a new life” request form; it’s a request, not a booking, and Kim replies if it’s a fit.

How long do requests take?

There’s no set turnaround. Kim sews alongside a full-time job, so she batches replies.

How do I care for an upcycled piece?

Cold wash inside out or hand wash, line dry, and mend small holes early. Each piece’s care label notes anything special.

Do you take fabric donations?

Ask first on Instagram — storage is limited, so Kim may not be able to accept everything.
