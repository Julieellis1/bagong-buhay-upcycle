About

Hi, it’s Kim.

Bagong Buhay is a slow-fashion clothing brand focused on one-of-one, upcycled clothes, made in the Portland area.

The name

Bagong buhay means “new life” in Tagalog — Kim’s mission is to give old materials new life.

How it started

Kim started the business in 2025, after deciding it was finally time, and has spent the seasons since behind market tables meeting the Portland making community. She still balances a full-time job with sewing, which is exactly why everything here happens slowly and in small batches.

The rules she sews by

  • 100% second-hand fabric.
  • Small batches, one of each.
  • Mends and seams stay visible. They’re part of the story.
  • Scraps become patches, panels and the next idea.

“estate sales, Goodwill, friends’ closets, a 2nd-hand Kimono shop in Japan.”
